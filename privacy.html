Effective September 29, 2026. Macro Usage Exporter for Confluence is published by Nathan Charles Acree, sole proprietor, using UtilityStack Labs as an informal brand. Business and legal notice address: 1 Xebec Rd, Palmyra, VA 22963, United States. For privacy questions, contact privacy@utilitystacklabs.com.

Information the app uses

When a signed-in user runs a report, the app uses the macro key and optional space keys they enter to search Confluence as that user. It uses matching page IDs, titles, space keys, last-updated values and links to show a page-level report and create a CSV. Search responses may also contain account or author metadata and other fields; the app discards them rather than adding them to report rows or CSV files. It does not request page bodies. Customer-controlled titles and other metadata may themselves contain personal information.

Where information goes

Search requests and result metadata pass through Atlassian Forge and Confluence to the user's browser. The app declares no external report-data egress or remote service. It has no UtilityStack Labs backend, app database or Forge storage for reports. Its code does not intentionally log report rows, search keys or credentials. Atlassian may process platform logs under its own policies.

Separately from reports, we receive the contact details, messages and attachments you choose to send for support, security or privacy help. Please do not send page bodies, CSV reports, credentials or sensitive content unless necessary and a suitable channel has been arranged. Atlassian may also make customer, site, licensing, sales, billing or other Marketplace business records available to us as the publisher. We use records actually made available to operate the listing, support customers, reconcile licensing and payments, and meet applicable obligations.

Retention and user choices

Report rows remain temporarily in the active browser page until it is closed or the report is cleared or replaced. A CSV is created in the browser only after a complete scan. A downloaded copy remains on the user's device under their organization's controls; we cannot remotely delete or revoke it. We have no retained report database to search, export or erase.

Ordinary support messages and customer-provided attachments may be retained for up to 12 months after the last support interaction and deleted when no longer needed. This is a review-and-deletion practice, not an automatic deletion feature. Security, fraud, dispute, accounting or tax, and legally required records may be kept longer where reasonably necessary or legally required. Marketplace business-record retention depends on applicable accounting, tax, dispute and legal requirements; we do not claim a fixed period for those records.

Permissions

The app requests only search:confluence. Confluence permissions determine which pages the signed-in user can find. A zero-result report does not prove that restricted or unindexed pages do not exist.
