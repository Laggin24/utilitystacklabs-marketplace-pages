Support hours

Monday–Friday, 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Eastern Time (America/New_York), excluding U.S. federal holidays. Our target response time is 48 hours. This is a response target, not a guaranteed resolution time or a 24/7 commitment.

Run a comparison

  1. Open Apps → Fix Version Drift in Jira.
  2. Enter the key of one company-managed Jira Software project you can view.
  3. Select Check versions. The app searches visible subtasks outside the Done status category and compares their Fix Version ID sets with their direct parents.
  4. Review differences and comparisons marked Not comparable. Differences may be intentional; the app does not change Jira.
  5. After the scan completes, select Download comparison CSV. The CSV includes all scanned subtasks, including matches and unknown comparisons. The on-screen matching-row toggle does not limit the CSV.

Version names and order do not determine equality: version ID sets do. Two empty sets match. One empty set, different sets and unavailable data have distinct outcomes. A parent that cannot be read is not treated as an empty set.

Coverage and limits

The normal scan supports up to 5,000 visible open subtasks, with a 100-page limit per search traversal. The screen shows at most 200 selected rows. Jira permissions and app-access rules determine what can be returned. Search indexing and changes during the scan may affect results; this is a scan window, not an atomic snapshot or proof that inaccessible work does not exist.

No complete CSV is offered after cancellation, failed requests, inconsistent pagination or an exceeded limit. Cancellation stops subsequent reads and discards the unfinished report; a request already sent may finish. For rate limits, follow the displayed retry timing and try later. Projects beyond the supported limit cannot be fully reported by this release.

For a bug report, send the app version, error text and displayed counts. Avoid sending CSVs, version names, issue content, credentials or tokens unless needed and a suitable channel has been arranged. Downloaded files remain under your organization's control.
