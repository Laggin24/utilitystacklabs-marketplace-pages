The Bonterms standard text applies only if this Attachment and these Details are expressly incorporated into the customer-provider Main Agreement.

Main Agreement date and DPA Effective Date: the date of that Customer's first Order for this app, as provided in Main Agreement Section 1.2, provided the DPA and these Details are incorporated in the Provider-Specific Terms applicable to that Order. No existing customer agreement is claimed.

Customer / data exporter: the organization or person placing that Order. Its legal name, address and account/notice contact are identified by the applicable Order and Marketplace account, or by a later contact it designates to Provider. Its relevant activities are operating the Confluence site and directing its users' reports. Its role is controller or processor, as applicable to the Customer Personal Data and its relationship with its own data subjects.

Business/legal notice address: 1 Xebec Rd, Palmyra, VA 22963, United States. Data-protection and legal contact: privacy@utilitystacklabs.com; security-incident contact: security@utilitystacklabs.com. Provider acts as processor for Customer-directed report processing.

Processing description

Service, subject matter and purpose: on a signed-in user's request, search Confluence for pages containing one exact macro key, optionally filtered by up to 20 space keys; display matching page-level metadata; and allow CSV export only after the scan completes. This is read-only. The app does not edit pages, schedule scans, request page bodies, use an external report backend, or retain report contents in an app database.

Categories of data subjects (Bonterms selections): Users; Employees and other personnel; Customer's business contacts; and Other people identifiable in Customer-controlled page titles or metadata. No claim is made that every Customer has all of these categories. Categories of Customer Personal Data (Bonterms selections): Account information (transient Confluence account/author metadata); user-generated content (page titles and identifying macro/space search inputs); unique identifiers (page IDs); and Other: space keys, last-updated timestamps, page links and site URL. The app discards account/author metadata rather than including it in report rows or CSV. Customer-controlled titles can contain names or other personal information.

Sensitive/special-category data: None/Not Applicable for the Service's intended scope. Such data is not requested or needed, and Main Agreement Section 7.2 prohibits Customer submission of Sensitive Data to the Cloud Service. This selection does not guarantee that a Customer-controlled page title can never contain sensitive information contrary to intended use.

Frequency of transfer (Bonterms selection): Other — on demand when a user runs a report, not continuous. Nature and purpose: permission-scoped read, transient handling and display, and Customer-directed CSV generation to provide the Service and carry out Customer Instructions. Duration/retention: during an active report session and provision of the Service under the Main Agreement, and thereafter pursuant to Bonterms DPA Section 8. Provider retains no app report database. The Customer controls any downloaded CSV. Platform logs and publisher-controlled support/business records are addressed separately under applicable platform terms and the privacy policy.

Subprocessors, notices and security

Provider Subprocessor List and transfers: Atlassian Pty Ltd (Australia), which receives Customer Personal Data as needed for Forge hosting/compute and Confluence API delivery for the Service. Atlassian's current subprocessor list identifies its downstream providers, functions and processing locations; its corporate location is not a claim that all data is processed in Australia. GitHub Pages hosts public documentation, not Customer report processing. The app has no external report-data egress or app report database.

Subprocessor Notice Method: email to the Customer's Marketplace Order, billing or technical administrator contact, or a replacement data-protection contact the Customer has designated to Provider. Provider has subscribed to Atlassian's subprocessor-change notices. No notice delivery to a production Customer has yet been tested.

Technical and organizational security measures: Atlassian-hosted Forge and Confluence authentication; permission-scoped search as the signed-in user through api.asUser(); only the search:confluence scope; no external report backend or storage; input/CQL and cursor validation; bounded pagination; no CSV for incomplete scans; HTTPS site-URL validation; text-only result rendering; CSV formula neutralization; and no intentional logging of report rows, queries or credentials in app code. Atlassian platform measures are described in its published Forge materials. No separate certification, location, backup or deletion-automation claim is made.

Proposed Additional Term — not yet agreed

Solely for a new or replacement downstream subprocessor engaged by Atlassian Pty Ltd in providing Forge for the Service, and not engaged directly by Provider, Section 4.3's 30-day advance-notice minimum is replaced with a 7-calendar-day advance-notice minimum. Provider will maintain its subscription to Atlassian's subprocessor-change notices and notify affected Customers by the Subprocessor Notice Method as soon as reasonably practicable after receiving Atlassian's applicable notice and, in all cases, at least 7 calendar days before that downstream subprocessor begins Processing Customer Personal Data. For those Atlassian-controlled downstream changes only, the period in Section 4.4(a) for Customer to object in writing on reasonable data-protection grounds is 7 calendar days after Customer receives Provider's notice. Section 4.3's 30-day advance-notice period and Section 4.4(a)'s 30-day objection period remain unchanged for Subprocessors engaged directly by Provider. Section 4.4(b)'s resolution and termination remedy and the DPA's 48-hour Specified Notice Period for Security Incidents remain unchanged. This term does not limit mandatory EU Standard Contractual Clause, UK or Swiss rights or their precedence under the DPA.

This narrow downstream exception is proposed for final owner adoption; it is not currently a customer commitment. Atlassian's Forge DPA states that subscribed developers receive at least 14 days' upstream notice. The proposed 7-day customer advance notice is a firm Provider commitment, not a claim that delivery has been tested with a production Customer.
