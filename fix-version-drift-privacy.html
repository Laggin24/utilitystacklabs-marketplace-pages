Information used for a report

The signed-in user selects one project key. The app searches Jira as that user and uses project metadata to confirm company-managed Jira Software scope; issue/subtask IDs and keys; parent IDs; Fix Version IDs and names; and issue-type and status-category information to select and compare visible non-Done subtasks. Readable parent keys and version data come from a separate permission-scoped parent search. Jira responses may include incidental metadata; the resolver reconstructs the comparison response rather than returning raw issue objects.

The report and CSV contain scan start/end timestamps, the project key, subtask key, readable parent key, version IDs/names and comparison outcome. The app does not request descriptions, comments, attachments, email addresses or assignee fields. Customer-controlled version names and issue keys may nevertheless contain personal information. Sensitive or special-category information is outside the intended scope and should not be entered for use by this app.

Processing and storage

Report data passes through Jira and Atlassian-hosted Forge to the user's browser. The app uses api.asUser() and declares only read:jira-work, with no external report-data egress, remote backend, app database or Forge report storage. Its code does not intentionally log report rows, project queries, credentials, tokens or raw upstream error bodies. Atlassian may process platform logs under its own terms.

Report rows remain in the active page until cleared, replaced or closed. A CSV is generated in the browser only after a complete scan. Downloaded files remain under the customer's control; Provider cannot remotely delete them. Jira permissions and indexing affect coverage, and a complete scan does not establish that restricted, deleted or unindexed issues do not exist.

Support and publisher records

Separately, Provider receives contact details, messages and attachments people choose to send for support, privacy or security help. Please avoid sending customer reports or issue information unless necessary and a suitable channel has been arranged. Atlassian may make Marketplace licensing, sales, billing and related business records available to Provider. Records actually received are used to provide support, administer the listing, reconcile payments and meet applicable obligations.

Ordinary support messages and customer-provided attachments may be retained for up to 12 months after the last support interaction and deleted when no longer needed. Security, fraud, dispute, accounting/tax or legally required records may be retained longer where reasonably necessary or required. No fixed retention period is promised for all Marketplace business records.

Privacy requests

Email privacy@utilitystacklabs.com about information Provider actually controls. Provider will verify and handle applicable requests; no automatic deletion or legally inapplicable rights are promised. Customer-held CSVs and Atlassian-controlled records may require contacting the customer or Atlassian. GitHub Pages hosts this documentation, and its practices apply to website visits.

The proposed customer DPA remains non-effective pending adoption and incorporation into the app's customer agreement.
