UtilityStack Labs / Security

Security reporting

This page applies to Atlassian Marketplace apps and related services that UtilityStack Labs actually operates, including Macro Usage Exporter for Confluence and Approval Reports for Jira Service Management. It does not describe or authorize testing of Atlassian's services or customer sites.

Report a vulnerability

Email security@utilitystacklabs.com with the affected app, a concise description, the possible impact, and steps to reproduce using your own or synthetic data. You may include redacted evidence. Do not include credentials, customer CSVs, page contents, or other sensitive data in the initial report; we can discuss an appropriate way to share additional information if needed.

We will review reports and respond as reasonably possible. We do not promise a particular acknowledgement or resolution time. Please coordinate public disclosure with us so we can investigate and, where appropriate, address the issue before details are released.

Scope and claims

This is a reporting contact, not a bug-bounty program or an invitation to test systems without permission. UtilityStack Labs does not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, CAIQ, penetration-test, or other certifications through this page.
